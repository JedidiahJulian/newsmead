## Study context and criteria

The planned population is 3–5 English-reading Filipino adults aged 45+ meeting the existing study eligibility criteria. The formative purpose is setup/calibration usability, ordinary news-reading interaction, comfort, visual-aid feedback and collection practicality. Two articles and 800–1,200 combined words remain proposed planning choices, not frozen protocol requirements or validated minimum exposure.

The review considered publication recency; complete traceable English text; sentence structure; jargon, number and proper-name load; self-contained text without mandatory images/charts; topic diversity; potential reading/eye/attention-related framing; and workload. Participant familiarity, interest, comprehension and comfort cannot be established by desk review.

### A. From small towns to big markets: La Union trade fair turns silver

Local products, food, crafts and small businesses; a coherent feature with subheadings and enough material for a longer reading.

Considerations: Moderate business vocabulary, proper names and acronyms (MSMEs, OTOP, AGLAUN). Positive/promotional government framing.

### B. Bohol town trades rice for plastic waste as LGUs turn trash into building materials

Concrete examples of plastic-for-rice exchange, recycled building materials and bags; text explains the processes without needing its photo.

Considerations: Moderate policy/numerical load and terms such as non-load-bearing cladding and petroleum coke; several initiatives rather than one narrative.

### C. Vigan City educates young children on road safety practices

Concrete public-safety reporting about traffic rules and a community activity; comparatively ordinary vocabulary.

Considerations: Local names and office acronyms (PSTMO/CSWDO); repetitive topic; adult news about children, with participant interest still unmeasured.

### D. Carindan Lake eyed as ‘Burnham Park’ attraction in Occidental Mindoro

A coherent planned-tourism report with concrete activities, little numerical detail and no required chart or photograph.

Considerations: Longer sentences and official phrases such as memorandum of agreement; relatively short total reading task.

### E. DTI shares holiday safety tips, smart spending advice for local buyers

Timely household-shopping topic, clear practical content and low numerical load; compact text.

Considerations: Certification labels PS/ICC/QR and technical words such as holographic and pyrotechnic; short task may offer less sustained reading.

### F. Agusan Sur taps farm tourism to expand local tour destination

A longer report connecting farms, visitors and livelihood; concrete example of an eco farm among institutional reporting.

Considerations: Higher institutional-language load, long titles and sentences (maximum 55 words); possible familiarity advantage for readers with agricultural experience remains unmeasured.

## Administration considerations after choice

- Use the same selected pair and app settings across participants, with order alternated where practical. Record actual order; a round of 3 or 5 participants cannot be exactly balanced across two orders, and changing order does not eliminate carryover.
- Record prior reading, topic familiarity, perceived difficulty and interest separately, preferably after each article so these questions do not steer natural reading.
- Record assistance, task completion, interruptions, breaks, discomfort, unavailable tracking and exposure for each participant/article. Do not infer reading accuracy from tracking eligibility or infer noticing from rendering.
- Preserve natural-reading feedback before the deliberate aid preview. Keep preview-only reactions distinguishable from actual natural aid presentation.
- If content questions are added, specify them and their answer keys before sessions as an explicit procedure addition. This review adds no comprehension test or eligibility cutoff.
- Trial the complete session agenda within the existing 45–60-minute plan. Do not extend reading solely to provoke aid activation. Follow the existing break/withdrawal rules.

## Measurement and interpretation limits

Body counts exclude title/byline/date/captions/footer/reporter credits. In-body headings count toward reading time; sentence summaries exclude those headings. En/em dashes separate tokens and hyphenated words count once. Counts and timing calculations were checked independently; sentence segmentation was manually reviewed. Full texts and metric details are in output/formative-article-shortlist-2026-11-16/{candidate-analysis.json,expanded-text-analysis.json}.

100/150/200wpm values are scenarios; actual participant rates are unknown and time estimates exclude scrolling, pauses and questions. These analyses are descriptive and do not validate a reading grade, demonstrate matched article difficulty, or predict individual comprehension.

All retained articles are from the Philippine Information Agency. Their pages carry a public-domain notice unless otherwise stated; source/author/date should be preserved with final materials. One publisher and generally positive government-project framing limit outlet/tone diversity. All selected bodies/quotations are in English, with local place/organization names and some Filipino vocabulary used as names or terms.
